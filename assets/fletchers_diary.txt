Kept fletching for the whole claim through the bad year. Writing down what the trade taught me, for whoever holds out here next.
\0Read the grain before the knife. Split your shafts along it, never across, and a single length of wood gives five true shafts where a careless hand hacks out three.

Knap the points small. A little stone flies straighter than a big one, and one stone knaps into more points than you would credit.
\0Set the feathering at a slant so the arrow spins. A spinning arrow holds its line in wind.

Same wood, same stone, five arrows where there were three. The difference is all in the reading.

- J.W., fletcher
