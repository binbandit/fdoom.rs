A trapper's coat is cut in the field, by firelight, with cold fingers. The pattern has to be lean or it does not get made at all.
\0Cut your furs on the diagonal and they lie into each other like shingles on a roof. The overlap does the sealing - you need four good furs, not five, and the wind still cannot find a way in.
\0Run one cord through the lot in a single drawstring, hem to collar. One cord, drawn snug, where a town tailor would baste in two.

Four furs, one cord, and a coat that has kept me alive north of the treeline for nine winters.
