A loose wick eats a torch from the inside. Most folk split a billet in two, wind it fat, and wonder why the dark comes early.
\0Split the wood fine - three staves from the billet, not two. Wind each wick tight as you can bear, then work wet coal dust into the winding and let it set.

A tight wick sips where a loose one gulps. Three torches from the same wood and coal, and each burns honest.
