Third winter tanning for the diggings, and I still see new hands lacing hides to a cord frame like their fathers taught them. Waste of good cord.
\0The bark does the binding. Strip wattle bark, steep it in the soak-pit until the water runs dark as strong tea, and put your hides down in it.

The liquor draws the hide tight all on its own. No frame, no cord - the tannin stiffens the skin as it cures.
\0Two hides in the pit, two leathers out, and your cord stays in your pocket where it belongs.

- The Tanner at Miller's Flat
